# Ironsworn Moves Reference

Moves are the structured mechanics of Ironsworn. Not every action is a move — moves trigger when their specific fiction is present. When uncertain, ask: "does the fiction actually match this move's trigger?"

*(Ironsworn is by Shawn Tomkin, released under CC BY 4.0. This is a compact reference; the full PDF is free from his site and is the authoritative source.)*

## Action Roll Basics

- Roll 1d6 (action die) + relevant stat + adds (momentum is not an add, but can be burned).
- Compare vs. 2d10 (challenge dice).
- **Strong hit**: action score > both challenge dice.
- **Weak hit**: action score > one challenge die.
- **Miss**: action score ≤ both challenge dice.
- **Match**: challenge dice show the same value — something notable happens (GM interprets tone).

## Core Stats
- **Edge** — speed, agility, ranged and finesse
- **Heart** — courage, empathy, social moves
- **Iron** — physical force, close combat, endurance
- **Shadow** — stealth, deceit, intellect at the edges
- **Wits** — observation, knowledge, reasoning

## Adventure Moves

### Face Danger
*Trigger:* You attempt something risky or react to peril.
Roll +stat (whichever best matches the approach).
- Strong: You do it. Take +1 momentum.
- Weak: You do it, but at a cost.
- Miss: Pay the price.

### Secure an Advantage
*Trigger:* You prepare, maneuver, or make an effort to gain position or leverage.
Roll +stat.
- Strong: +2 momentum.
- Weak: +1 momentum.
- Miss: Pay the price.

### Gather Information
*Trigger:* You search, investigate, interview, or study.
Roll +Wits.
- Strong: You learn something useful and specific. +2 momentum.
- Weak: You learn something, but it's incomplete or raises new questions. +1 momentum.
- Miss: Pay the price — the info is wrong, you draw unwanted attention, or you find something you wish you hadn't.

### Heal
*Trigger:* You use mundane means to aid yourself or a companion.
Roll +Iron or +Wits.
- Strong: Restore +2 health (+1 to companion too, if applicable). +1 momentum.
- Weak: Restore +1 health. Out of supply, or it takes longer than hoped.
- Miss: Pay the price.

### Resupply
*Trigger:* You hunt, forage, or scavenge to restock.
Roll +Wits.
- Strong: +2 supply.
- Weak: +1 supply, but it cost time or invited trouble.
- Miss: Pay the price.

### Make Camp
*Trigger:* You settle in for a long rest in the wild.
Roll +Supply.
- Strong: Choose 3 benefits: recuperate (+health), partake (+spirit), relax (clear a debility), focus (+1 momentum), prepare (next journey roll +1).
- Weak: Choose 2.
- Miss: Something goes wrong at camp. Pay the price, or face a threat.

### Undertake a Journey
*Trigger:* You travel across a long distance. Set a progress track with rank.
Each waypoint: Roll +Wits.
- Strong: Mark progress + 1 on track, and +1 supply.
- Weak: Mark progress on track. No supply.
- Miss: Pay the price, reveal a danger or setback.
End: Roll for Reach Your Destination using the progress track.

### Reach Your Destination
*Trigger:* You arrive at your journey's end.
Roll progress: Does the filled progress beat both challenge dice?
- Strong: You arrive favorably — good intel, or find something helpful.
- Weak: Complication awaits at the destination.
- Miss: Your arrival is unwelcome or the destination is not as expected.

## Combat Moves

### Enter the Fray
*Trigger:* Combat begins.
Roll +Heart (you engage in a situation you didn't start), +Shadow (ambush), or +Wits (prepared).
- Strong: In control. +2 momentum.
- Weak: In control, +1 momentum, but you took a risk or lost something.
- Miss: In a bad spot.

### Strike
*Trigger:* You attack while in control.
Roll +Iron or +Edge.
- Strong: Inflict +2 harm (weapon damage). +1 momentum.
- Weak: Inflict +1 harm, but you lose control of the fight.
- Miss: Pay the price. You are now in a bad spot.

### Clash
*Trigger:* You fight while in a bad spot.
Roll +Iron or +Edge.
- Strong: Inflict damage and regain control. +1 momentum.
- Weak: Inflict damage but remain in a bad spot.
- Miss: Pay the price — take damage, lose position, face a new threat.

### Battle
*Trigger:* You engage in a fight where the outcome is a foregone conclusion.
Roll +stat matching your approach.
- Strong: Decisive win. Take +2 momentum.
- Weak: Win at cost.
- Miss: Defeat. Pay the price.

### End the Fight
*Trigger:* You finish a combat you've been tracking with a progress track.
Roll progress: Does filled progress beat both challenge dice?
- Strong: Your intended outcome (kill, capture, drive off) happens cleanly.
- Weak: Outcome happens but at a cost — or the foe reveals something unexpected.
- Miss: Your foe prevails or escapes. Pay the price.

## Vow Moves

### Swear an Iron Vow
*Trigger:* You make a sworn promise, or begin a quest.
Set rank: troublesome / dangerous / formidable / extreme / epic.
Roll +Heart.
- Strong: +2 momentum. You are emboldened.
- Weak: +1 momentum, but the path ahead is unclear.
- Miss: Pay the price. If you still swear the vow, do so despite misgivings.

### Reach a Milestone
*Trigger:* You meaningfully advance your vow.
Mark progress on the vow's track per its rank (no roll).

### Fulfill Your Vow
*Trigger:* You believe your vow is complete.
Roll progress: filled boxes vs. 2d10.
- Strong: Fulfilled. Gain experience equal to rank.
- Weak: Fulfilled, but with complication — a new vow may emerge, or someone has a grievance.
- Miss: The vow is not what you thought. Reforge it (downgrade? redirect?) or forsake it.

### Forsake Your Vow
*Trigger:* You abandon a vow.
Suffer shaken or take -momentum. This is a heavy moment — narrate what breaks inside the character.

## Relationship Moves

### Compel
*Trigger:* You try to persuade, bargain, intimidate, or deceive.
Roll +Heart (persuade/inspire), +Iron (intimidate), or +Shadow (deceive).
- Strong: They agree or yield. +1 momentum.
- Weak: They agree conditionally. Pick a condition.
- Miss: Pay the price. Relationship damage likely.

### Sojourn
*Trigger:* You spend time in a community.
Roll +Heart.
- Strong: Choose 3 benefits (clear conditions, +supply, gather info, forge bonds, etc.).
- Weak: Choose 2.
- Miss: Something goes wrong. Pay the price.

### Forge a Bond
*Trigger:* You try to create a meaningful connection with an NPC/community.
Roll +Heart (with bond threshold per official rules).
- Strong: Bond forged, +1 bond.
- Weak: Bond forged but complicated.
- Miss: Pay the price.

## Suffer Moves (condensed)

When the player pays the price:
- **Endure Harm** (take damage) — roll +Iron or +health
- **Endure Stress** (emotional damage) — roll +Heart or +spirit
- **Companion Takes a Hit** — if a companion is with them
- **Face Desolation** (health at 0 and harm still coming) — roll +heart
- **Face Defeat** (a track is full against them, or narrative requires it)

## "Ask the Oracle" (yes/no)
When Mythic GME is loaded, use the Fate Check there instead. Otherwise:
- Almost certain / likely / 50/50 / unlikely / small chance — the GM picks a likelihood and rolls 2d10. If under the threshold, yes.
- Common thresholds (2d10 <=): AC 90 / Likely 75 / 50/50 50 / Unlikely 25 / Small 10.
