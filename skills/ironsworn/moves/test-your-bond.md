# Test Your Bond

**Trigger:** Your bond with a person or community is tested through conflict, betrayal, or difficult circumstance.

**Roll:** +Heart

---

## Strong Hit

This test has strengthened your bond.

**Player chooses one:**
1. Take **+1 spirit**.
2. Take **+2 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

Your bond is fragile. You must prove your loyalty. Envision what they ask (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

Do it — or Swear an Iron Vow → `moves/swear-an-iron-vow.md` to do it.

If you refuse or fail: clear the bond and **Pay the Price** → `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

## Miss

Or if you have no interest in maintaining this relationship: clear the bond.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
