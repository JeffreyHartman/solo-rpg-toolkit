# Endure Stress

**Trigger:** You face mental shock or despair.

**Mechanics before rolling:**
1. Suffer -spirit equal to your foe's rank or as appropriate to the situation.
2. If spirit drops to 0, suffer -momentum equal to any remaining -spirit (the overflow).
3. Update Character.md immediately before rolling.

**Roll:** +spirit or +Heart, whichever is higher.

---

## Strong Hit

**Player chooses one:**
1. Shake it off: If spirit > 0, suffer **-1 momentum** in exchange for **+1 spirit**.
2. Embrace the darkness: Take **+1 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

You press on. No additional effect.

## Miss

Suffer **-1 momentum** additionally.

If spirit is at 0, mark **Shaken** or **Corrupted** (if currently unmarked). If both are already marked, roll d100 on the table below.

---

## Endure Stress Table (d100) — spirit at 0, both debilities marked

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | You are overwhelmed. **Face Desolation** → `moves/face-desolation.md` |
| 11–25 | You give up. **Forsake Your Vow** → `moves/forsake-your-vow.md` (one relevant to your current crisis, if possible) |
| 26–50 | You give in to a fear or compulsion, and act against your better instincts. |
| 51–100 | You persevere. |

---

**Debility tracking:** When you mark Shaken or Corrupted `[x]`, reduce max momentum by 1 and reset by 1. Update the `_Momentum bounds:` line in Character.md immediately.
