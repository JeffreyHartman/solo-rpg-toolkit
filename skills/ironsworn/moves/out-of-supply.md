# Out of Supply

**Trigger:** Your supply is exhausted — reaches 0.

**No roll.** This is a passive consequence.

**Immediate effect:** Mark the **Unprepared** debility `[x]` in Character.md. Reduce max momentum by 1 and reset by 1.

**Ongoing while Unprepared:** If you suffer additional -supply (supply is already at 0), exchange each additional -supply for any combination of -health, -spirit, or -momentum as appropriate to the circumstances.

---

Getting supply back clears Unprepared — mark it unchecked and restore the momentum bounds.
