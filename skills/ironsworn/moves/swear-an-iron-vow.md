# Swear an Iron Vow

**Trigger:** You swear upon iron to complete a quest.

**Setup:**
1. Write the vow. It should be concrete — a specific promise to do a specific thing.
2. Set rank: Troublesome / Dangerous / Formidable / Extreme / Epic.
3. Create a vow progress track (10 boxes) — in Character.md Vows callout AND as a Thread file.

**Roll:** +Heart. If you make this vow to a person or community with whom you share a bond, add **+1**.

---

## Strong Hit

You are emboldened and the path ahead is clear (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Take **+2 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

You are determined but begin with more questions than answers. Take **+1 momentum**. Envision what you must do to find a path forward.

## Miss

You face a significant obstacle before you can even begin. Envision what stands in your way (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

**Player chooses one:**
1. You press on: Suffer **-2 momentum**, and do what you must to overcome this obstacle.
2. You give up: **Forsake Your Vow** → `moves/forsake-your-vow.md`

---

**Thread file format:** After creating the Thread file, insert the progress table block immediately after the metadata — see SKILL.md vow overlay section for the exact format.
