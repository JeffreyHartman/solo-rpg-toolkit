# Battle

**Trigger:** You fight a battle where the details happen in a blur and the outcome is what matters.

Use this instead of the full Enter the Fray / Strike / Clash / End the Fight sequence when the encounter is not the narrative focus.

**Roll:** based on your primary approach:
- Fight at range, using speed and terrain → **+Edge**
- Fight depending on courage, allies, or companions → **+Heart**
- Fight in close to overpower opponents → **+Iron**
- Fight using trickery to befuddle opponents → **+Shadow**
- Fight using careful tactics to outsmart opponents → **+Wits**

---

## Strong Hit

You achieve your objective unconditionally. Take **+2 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

You achieve your objective, but not without cost.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

## Miss

You are defeated and the objective is lost.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
