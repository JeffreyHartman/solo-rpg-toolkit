# Face Danger

**Trigger:** You attempt something risky or react to an imminent threat.

**Roll:** +stat matching your approach:
- Speed, agility, or precision → **+Edge**
- Charm, loyalty, or courage → **+Heart**
- Aggressive action, forceful defense, strength, or endurance → **+Iron**
- Deception, stealth, or trickery → **+Shadow**
- Expertise, insight, or observation → **+Wits**

---

## Strong Hit

You succeed. Take **+1 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

You succeed, but face a troublesome cost.

**Present these options — player chooses one:**
1. You are delayed, lose advantage, or face a new danger: Suffer **-1 momentum**.
2. You are tired or hurt: **Endure Harm** (1 harm) → `moves/endure-harm.md`
3. You are dispirited or afraid: **Endure Stress** (1 stress) → `moves/endure-stress.md`
4. You sacrifice resources: Suffer **-1 supply**.

Do not choose for the player. Do not narrate a cost without the player selecting it.

## Miss

You fail, or your progress is undermined by a dramatic and costly turn of events.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
