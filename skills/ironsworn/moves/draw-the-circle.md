# Draw the Circle

**Trigger:** You challenge someone to a formal duel, or accept a challenge.

**Roll:** +Heart. If you share a bond with this community, add **+1**.

---

## Strong Hit

Take **+1 momentum**. You may also choose up to **two boasts** and take +1 momentum for each boast chosen.

## Weak Hit

You may choose **one boast** in exchange for +1 momentum.

**Available boasts (each grants +1 momentum but imposes a handicap on you):**
1. **Grant first strike:** Your foe has initiative at the start.
2. **Bare yourself:** Take no benefit of armor or shield; foe's harm is +1.
3. **Hold no iron:** Take no benefit of weapons; your harm is 1.
4. **Bloody yourself:** Endure Harm (1 harm) → `moves/endure-harm.md`
5. **To the death:** This fight must end with one party dead.

## Miss

You begin at a disadvantage. Your foe has initiative.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

---

Then resolve the fight using combat moves. **If you are the victor:** you may make a lawful demand, and your opponent must comply or forfeit their honor and standing. **If you refuse, surrender, or are defeated:** they make a demand of you.
