# Strike

**Trigger:** You have initiative and attack.

**Roll:**
- Close quarters → **+Iron**
- At range → **+Edge**

---

## Strong Hit

Inflict **+1 harm** (weapon damage + 1). You retain initiative.

## Weak Hit

Inflict your harm. You lose initiative (foe now has it).

## Miss

Your attack fails.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`. Your foe has initiative.

---

**Harm inflicted:** Mark progress on the foe's combat track (progress per harm = their rank). Weapon damage is typically 1-3 harm depending on the weapon; strong hit adds +1 on top.

Track is filled? When you make a decisive move and score a strong hit, you may roll **End the Fight** → `moves/end-the-fight.md`.
