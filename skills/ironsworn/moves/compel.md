# Compel

**Trigger:** You attempt to persuade someone to do something or share what they know.

**Roll:** based on your approach:
- Charm, pacify, barter, or convince → **+Heart** (add +1 if you share a bond with them)
- Threaten or incite → **+Iron**
- Lie or swindle → **+Shadow**

---

## Strong Hit

They do what you want or share what they know. Take **+1 momentum**.

If you use this exchange to Gather Information → `moves/gather-information.md`, make that move now and add +1.

## Weak Hit

They agree, but ask something of you in return. Envision what they want (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Then decide whether to comply.

## Miss

They refuse, or make a demand which costs you greatly.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`. Relationship damage is likely.
