# Resupply

**Trigger:** You hunt, forage, or scavenge to restock supplies.

**Roll:** +Wits

---

## Strong Hit

You bolster your resources. Take **+2 supply**.

## Weak Hit

You find some supplies, but it costs effort.

Take up to **+2 supply**, but suffer **-1 momentum for each** supply taken. Player decides how much supply to take (0, 1, or 2).

## Miss

You find nothing helpful.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
