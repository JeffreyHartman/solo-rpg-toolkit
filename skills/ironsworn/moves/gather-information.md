# Gather Information

**Trigger:** You search an area, ask questions, conduct an investigation, or follow a track.

**Roll:** +Wits. If acting within a community or asking questions of a person with whom you share a bond, add **+1**.

---

## Strong Hit

You discover something helpful and specific. The path forward or action needed is made clear. Envision what you learn (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Take **+2 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

The information complicates your quest or introduces a new danger. Envision what you discover (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Take **+1 momentum**.

## Miss

Your investigation unearths a dire threat or reveals an unwelcome truth that undermines your quest.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
