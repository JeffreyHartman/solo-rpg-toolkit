# Secure an Advantage

**Trigger:** You assess a situation, make preparations, or attempt to gain leverage before acting.

**Roll:** +stat matching your approach:
- Speed, agility, or precision → **+Edge**
- Charm, loyalty, or courage → **+Heart**
- Aggressive action, force, or endurance → **+Iron**
- Deception, stealth, or trickery → **+Shadow**
- Expertise, insight, or observation → **+Wits**

---

## Strong Hit

You gain advantage. **Player chooses one:**
1. Take control: Make another move now (not a progress move), and add **+1**.
2. Prepare to act: Take **+2 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

Your advantage is short-lived. Take **+1 momentum**.

## Miss

You fail or your assumptions betray you.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
