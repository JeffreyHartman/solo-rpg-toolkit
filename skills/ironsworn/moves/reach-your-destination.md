# Reach Your Destination

**Trigger:** The player decides their journey is complete and wants to resolve it.

**Progress roll.** Roll challenge dice and compare to the filled progress boxes on the journey track. **Momentum is ignored on this roll.**

Do not use a stat. Do not add modifiers. The number of filled boxes is the score.

---

## Strong Hit

The situation at your destination favors you.

**Player chooses one:**
1. Make another move now (not a progress move), and add +1.
2. Take **+1 momentum**.

Narrate a favorable arrival.

## Weak Hit

You arrive but face an unforeseen hazard or complication. Envision what you find (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

Narrate the arrival, present the complication, hand back control.

## Miss

You have gone hopelessly astray, your objective is lost, or you were misled about your destination.

If the journey continues:
- Clear all but one filled progress box.
- Raise the journey's rank by one (if not already Epic).

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

---

Journey is now resolved. Close the journey progress track in Character.md and the Thread file (if one exists).
