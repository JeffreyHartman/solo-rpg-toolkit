# Make Camp

**Trigger:** You rest and recover for several hours in the wild.

**Roll:** +Supply

---

## Strong Hit

Choose **two** benefits from the list below.

## Weak Hit

Choose **one** benefit from the list below.

**Available benefits:**
1. **Recuperate:** Take +1 health for you and any companions.
2. **Partake:** Suffer -1 supply and take +1 health for you and any companions.
3. **Relax:** Take +1 spirit.
4. **Focus:** Take +1 momentum.
5. **Prepare:** When you break camp, add +1 if you Undertake a Journey → `moves/undertake-a-journey.md`.

Present the list and wait for player to choose.

## Miss

You take no comfort. Something goes wrong at camp.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
