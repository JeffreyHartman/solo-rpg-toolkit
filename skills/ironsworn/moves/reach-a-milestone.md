# Reach a Milestone

**Trigger:** You make significant progress toward your vow by overcoming a critical obstacle, completing a perilous journey, solving a complex mystery, defeating a powerful threat, gaining vital support, or acquiring a crucial item.

**No roll.** Just mark progress on the vow's track.

**Progress per milestone by rank:**

| Rank | Progress marked |
|---|---|
| Troublesome | 3 boxes |
| Dangerous | 2 boxes |
| Formidable | 1 box |
| Extreme | 2 ticks (½ box) |
| Epic | 1 tick (¼ box) |

Update both Character.md (vow line) and the Thread file progress table.

---

Not every minor success is a milestone. A milestone requires genuine, meaningful advancement against the core obstacle of the vow. When in doubt, ask: "Does this change what the quest requires?"
