# Face Death

**Trigger:** You are brought to the brink of death — health is 0 and harm is still incoming, or fiction demands it.

**Roll:** +Heart

---

## Strong Hit

Death rejects you. You are cast back into the mortal world.

## Weak Hit

**Player chooses one:**
1. You die, but not before making a noble sacrifice. Envision your final moments. (Character death.)
2. Death desires something of you in exchange for your life. Envision what it wants (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Swear an Iron Vow → `moves/swear-an-iron-vow.md` (Formidable or Extreme) to complete that quest. You return to the mortal world — but you are now **Cursed**. You may only clear Cursed by completing the quest. If you fail to score a hit when you swear the vow, or refuse it, you are dead.

## Miss

You are dead.

---

This is character death territory. Present the outcome honestly — do not soften a miss.
