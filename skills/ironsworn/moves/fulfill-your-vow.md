# Fulfill Your Vow

**Trigger:** You believe your vow is complete.

**Progress roll.** Roll challenge dice and compare to filled progress on the vow's track. **Momentum is ignored on this roll.**

---

## Strong Hit

Your quest is complete.

Mark experience:
| Rank | Experience |
|---|---|
| Troublesome | 1 |
| Dangerous | 2 |
| Formidable | 3 |
| Extreme | 4 |
| Epic | 5 |

Close the vow progress track. Archive the Thread file.

## Weak Hit

There is more to be done, or you realize the truth of your quest. Envision what you discover (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

Mark experience (one less than above per rank — Troublesome=0, Dangerous=1, Formidable=2, Extreme=3, Epic=4).

You may Swear an Iron Vow → `moves/swear-an-iron-vow.md` to set things right. If you do, add +1.

## Miss

Your quest is undone. Envision what happens (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

**Player chooses one:**
1. You recommit: Clear all but one filled progress box, and raise the vow's rank by one (if not already Epic).
2. You give up: **Forsake Your Vow** → `moves/forsake-your-vow.md`
