# Undertake a Journey

**Trigger:** You travel across hazardous or unfamiliar lands.

**THIS IS A PROGRESS TRACK MOVE.** Each roll marks one waypoint. The journey ends only when the player chooses to roll Reach Your Destination. A single strong hit does not mean arrival.

**Roll:** +Wits

---

## Setup (Before First Roll)

1. Establish the journey rank. Rank = difficulty of the journey, not encounter hazards along the way.
   - Short local road → Troublesome
   - Regional travel with real danger → Dangerous
   - Major expedition through hostile territory → Formidable
   - Cross-continent or epic quest → Extreme / Epic
2. Create the journey progress track (10 boxes). Note rank.
3. If setting off from a community with which you share a bond, add +1 to the initial roll.

**Progress per waypoint:**

| Rank | Progress marked |
|---|---|
| Troublesome | 3 boxes |
| Dangerous | 2 boxes |
| Formidable | 1 box |
| Extreme | 2 ticks (½ box) |
| Epic | 1 tick (¼ box) |

---

## Strong Hit

You reach a waypoint. If unknown, envision it (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

**Player chooses one:**
1. Make good use of resources: Mark progress.
2. Move at speed: Mark progress AND take **+1 momentum**, but suffer **-1 supply**.

Narrate the waypoint briefly. Then stop and hand back: *"What do you do as you travel? Press on, or make camp?"*

## Weak Hit

You reach a waypoint. Mark progress. But suffer **-1 supply**.

Narrate the waypoint and the complication or cost. Hand back control.

## Miss

You are waylaid by a perilous event. **Do NOT mark progress.**

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

---

**One roll ≠ one journey.** Even a strong hit only marks one waypoint. Never narrate arrival at the destination after this roll. The player decides when to attempt Reach Your Destination.

When the player is ready: **Reach Your Destination** → `moves/reach-your-destination.md`
