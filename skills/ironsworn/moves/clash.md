# Clash

**Trigger:** Your foe has initiative and you fight back.

**Roll:**
- Fighting in close quarters → **+Iron**
- Exchanging volleys at range, or shooting at an advancing foe → **+Edge**

---

## Strong Hit

Inflict your harm. You have initiative.

**Player chooses one:**
1. Bolster your position: Take **+1 momentum**.
2. Find an opening: Inflict **+1 harm**.

## Weak Hit

Inflict your harm. But then —

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`. Your foe retains initiative.

## Miss

You are outmatched.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`. Your foe retains initiative.
