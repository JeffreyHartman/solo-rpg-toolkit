# Aid Your Ally

**Trigger:** You Secure an Advantage in direct support of an ally.

This is not a separate move — it is a special application of Secure an Advantage → `moves/secure-an-advantage.md`.

When you do, and score a hit, the **ally** (not you) takes the benefits of the move.

If you are in combat and score a **strong hit**, both you and your ally have initiative.
