# Face a Setback

**Trigger:** Your momentum is at its minimum (-6) and you suffer additional -momentum.

**No roll.** This is a passive consequence.

**Player chooses one for each additional -momentum:**

1. Exchange each -momentum for any combination of -health, -spirit, or -supply as appropriate to the circumstances.

2. Envision an event or discovery (Ask the Oracle if unsure) that undermines progress in a current quest, journey, or fight. Then for each additional -momentum, clear 1 unit of progress on that track per its rank:
   | Rank | Progress cleared |
   |---|---|
   | Troublesome | 3 progress |
   | Dangerous | 2 progress |
   | Formidable | 1 progress |
   | Extreme | 2 ticks |
   | Epic | 1 tick |

---

Present both options to the player before applying. Their choice shapes the narrative.
