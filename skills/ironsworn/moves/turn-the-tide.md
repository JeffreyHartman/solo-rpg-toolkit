# Turn the Tide

**Trigger:** Once per fight, when you risk it all to seize a critical moment.

**Usage:** Once per fight only. You steal initiative from your foe to make a move (not a progress move).

**Add +1** to that move. On a hit, take **+1 momentum**.

If you fail to score a hit on that move:

→ **Pay the Price** with a dire outcome. Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

---

Note this has been used when it triggers — it cannot be used again in the same fight.
