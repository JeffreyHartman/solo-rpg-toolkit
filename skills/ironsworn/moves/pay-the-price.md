# Pay the Price

**Trigger:** Any move results in a miss. Mandatory — not optional, not a narration prompt.

**No roll.** This is a procedure, not a passive outcome.

---

## STOP. Do Not Narrate Through the Consequence.

Pay the Price creates the next beat. It does not end it.

**Narrating "she grabs his arm and he stops" is not paying the price — it erases it.**

---

## Procedure

**Step 1 — Determine the price.** Choose one:
- Make the most obvious negative outcome happen.
- Envision two negative outcomes. Rate one "likely." Roll Ask the Oracle (yes/no). On "yes," that outcome happens; otherwise, the other.
- Roll d100 on the table below.

**Step 2 — State the price concretely.** Name what is happening or about to happen. Do NOT narrate it to resolution.

**Step 3 — STOP.** Then either:
- Trigger the appropriate follow-on move (see table below), OR
- Hand control back: *"What do you do?"*

If an NPC is in danger: the player decides whether to intervene and rolls for it. Do not have another character save them in narration.

---

## One Price Per Miss

Pay the Price = one consequence. Do not also drain momentum, supply, or any other resource separately unless that IS the chosen price.

> Journey miss → Pay the Price → Face Danger triggered = price paid. Full stop. No additional momentum penalty on top.

---

## Follow-On Moves by Price Type

| Price shape | Move to trigger |
|---|---|
| Physical injury | **Endure Harm** → `moves/endure-harm.md` |
| Emotional damage, despair | **Endure Stress** → `moves/endure-stress.md` |
| Companion injured | **Companion Endure Harm** → `moves/companion-endure-harm.md` |
| New immediate danger | **Face Danger** → `moves/face-danger.md` |
| Supply lost | Suffer -1 supply (update Character.md) |
| Momentum cost | Suffer -1 momentum (update Character.md) |
| Vow/quest undermined | Narrate complication; if severe, **Forsake Your Vow** → `moves/forsake-your-vow.md` |

Say the move name aloud: *"This is Face Danger."* Player can correct if you picked wrong.

---

## Pay the Price Table (d100)

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Roll again and apply that result but make it worse. If you get this again, think of something dreadful that changes the course of your quest and make it happen. |
| 3–5 | A person or community you trusted loses faith in you, or acts against you. |
| 6–9 | A person or community you care about is exposed to danger. |
| 10–16 | You are separated from something or someone. |
| 17–23 | Your action has an unintended effect. |
| 24–32 | Something of value is lost or destroyed. |
| 33–41 | The current situation worsens. |
| 42–50 | A new danger or foe is revealed. |
| 51–59 | It causes a delay or puts you at a disadvantage. |
| 60–68 | It is harmful. → Endure Harm |
| 69–76 | It is stressful. → Endure Stress |
| 77–85 | A surprising development complicates your quest. |
| 86–90 | It wastes resources. |
| 91–94 | It forces you to act against your best intentions. |
| 95–98 | A friend, companion, or ally is put in harm's way (or you are, if alone). |
| 99–100 | Roll twice more. Both results occur. If the same result, make it worse. |
