# Enter the Fray

**Trigger:** You enter into combat.

**Setup first:** Set the rank of each foe.

| Foe rank | Progress per harm inflicted on them | Harm they inflict |
|---|---|---|
| Troublesome | 3 progress | 1 harm |
| Dangerous | 2 progress | 2 harm |
| Formidable | 1 progress | 3 harm |
| Extreme | 2 ticks | 4 harm |
| Epic | 1 tick | 5 harm |

Create a combat progress track for each significant foe (10 boxes).

**Roll:** based on how combat begins:
- Facing off against your foe → **+Heart**
- Moving into position against unaware foe, or striking without warning → **+Shadow**
- Ambushed → **+Wits**

---

## Strong Hit

Take **+2 momentum**. You have initiative.

## Weak Hit

**Player chooses one:**
1. Bolster your position: Take **+2 momentum**.
2. Prepare to act: Take **initiative**.

## Miss

Combat begins with you at a disadvantage. Your foe has initiative.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

---

**Initiative matters.** With initiative: use Strike → `moves/strike.md`. Without: use Clash → `moves/clash.md`. Track who has initiative after each move.
