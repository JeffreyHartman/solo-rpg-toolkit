# Face Desolation

**Trigger:** You are brought to the brink of desolation — spirit is 0 and stress is still incoming, or fiction demands it.

**Roll:** +Heart

---

## Strong Hit

You resist and press on.

## Weak Hit

**Player chooses one:**
1. Your spirit breaks, but not before a noble sacrifice. Envision your final moments. (Character lost to desolation.)
2. You see a vision of a dreaded future event. Envision it (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Swear an Iron Vow → `moves/swear-an-iron-vow.md` (Formidable or Extreme) to prevent it. You return to your senses — but you are now **Tormented**. You may only clear Tormented by completing the quest. If you fail to score a hit when swearing, or refuse, you are lost.

## Miss

You succumb to despair or horror and are lost. (Character lost to desolation.)

---

Character loss to desolation is as final as death. Present the miss outcome honestly.
