# Sojourn

**Trigger:** You spend time in a community seeking assistance.

**Roll:** +Heart. If you share a bond with this community, add **+1**.

---

## Strong Hit

Choose **two** from the categories below (you and allies each).

## Weak Hit

Choose **one**. If you share a bond, choose one more.

**Clear a Condition:**
- **Mend:** Clear the Wounded debility and take +1 health.
- **Hearten:** Clear the Shaken debility and take +1 spirit.
- **Equip:** Clear the Unprepared debility and take +1 supply.

**Recover:**
- **Recuperate:** Take +2 health for yourself and any companions.
- **Consort:** Take +2 spirit.
- **Provision:** Take +2 supply.
- **Plan:** Take +2 momentum.

**Provide Aid:**
- **Take a quest:** Envision what this community needs (Ask the Oracle if unsure). If you help, Swear an Iron Vow → `moves/swear-an-iron-vow.md` and add +1.

---

## Focus Roll (optional, on a hit)

After choosing, you and allies may each **focus on one chosen recover action** and roll +Heart again (add +1 if bond). On a strong hit: take +2 more for that action. On a weak hit: take +1 more. On a miss: lose all benefits for that action.

---

## Miss

You find no help here.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
