# Heal

**Trigger:** You treat an injury or ailment for yourself or a companion.

**Roll:**
- Treating an ally → **+Wits**
- Treating yourself → **+Wits or +Iron, whichever is lower** (self-care is harder)

---

## Strong Hit

Your care is helpful. If the patient has the **Wounded** condition, you may clear it. Then take or give up to **+2 health**.

## Weak Hit

As above, but you must also suffer **-1 supply** OR **-1 momentum** (player's choice).

## Miss

Your aid is ineffective.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
