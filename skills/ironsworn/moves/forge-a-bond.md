# Forge a Bond

**Trigger:** You have spent significant time with a person or community, stood together to face hardships, or made sacrifices for their cause.

**Roll:** +Heart. If you make this move after successfully Fulfilling Your Vow to their benefit, you may **reroll any dice**.

---

## Strong Hit

Make note of the bond. Mark a tick on your bond progress track.

**Player chooses one:**
1. Take **+1 spirit**.
2. Take **+2 momentum**.

## Weak Hit

They ask something more of you first. Envision what it is (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Do it — or Swear an Iron Vow → `moves/swear-an-iron-vow.md` to do it. Then mark the bond.

If you refuse or fail: **Pay the Price** → `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

## Miss

They reject you.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.
