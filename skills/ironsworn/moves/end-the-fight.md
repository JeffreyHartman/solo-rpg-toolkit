# End the Fight

**Trigger:** You make a move to take decisive action and score a strong hit on that move. You may then choose to resolve the fight.

**Progress roll.** Roll challenge dice and compare to the filled progress on the foe's combat track. **Momentum is ignored on this roll.**

---

## Strong Hit

This foe is no longer in the fight. They are killed, out of action, flee, or surrender — as appropriate to the situation and your intent. Ask the Oracle if unsure.

## Weak Hit

The foe is out, but at a cost.

**Player chooses one:**
1. It's worse than you thought: **Endure Harm** → `moves/endure-harm.md`
2. You are overcome: **Endure Stress** → `moves/endure-stress.md`
3. Your victory is short-lived: A new danger or foe appears, or an existing danger worsens.
4. You suffer collateral damage: Something of value is lost or broken, or someone important must pay the cost.
5. You'll pay for it: An objective falls out of reach.
6. Others won't forget: You are marked for vengeance.

## Miss

You have lost this fight.

→ **Pay the Price.** Read `moves/pay-the-price.md`.

---

The foe's combat progress track is now resolved. Close it. If multiple foes remain, their tracks continue.
