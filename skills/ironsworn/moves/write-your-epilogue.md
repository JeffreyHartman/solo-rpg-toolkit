# Write Your Epilogue

**Trigger:** You retire from your life as Ironsworn. This is a campaign-ending move.

**Setup:** Envision two things — what you hope for, and what you fear.

**Progress roll.** Roll challenge dice and compare to your **bonds progress track**. **Momentum is ignored on this roll.**

---

## Strong Hit

Things come to pass as you hoped.

Narrate the character's epilogue — what becomes of them, who they are with, what they built.

## Weak Hit

Your life takes an unexpected turn, but not necessarily for the worse. You find yourself spending your days with someone or in a place you did not foresee. Envision it (Ask the Oracle if unsure).

Narrate an epilogue with that unexpected element.

## Miss

Your fears are realized.

Narrate the character's fate — they did not find what they sought, or what they feared came true.

---

This ends the campaign. Archive the character file and campaign folder.
