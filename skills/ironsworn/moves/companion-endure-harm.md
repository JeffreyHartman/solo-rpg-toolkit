# Companion Endure Harm

**Trigger:** Your companion faces physical damage.

**Mechanics before rolling:**
1. Suffer -health on the companion equal to harm inflicted.
2. If companion health drops to 0, exchange leftover -health for -momentum (overflow applies to you).
3. Update Character.md companion health immediately.

**Roll:** +Heart or +companion's current health, whichever is higher.

---

## Strong Hit

Your companion rallies. Give them **+1 health**.

## Weak Hit

Your companion is battered. If their health is 0, they cannot assist you until they gain at least +1 health.

## Miss

Suffer **-1 momentum** additionally.

If companion health is 0: they are gravely wounded and out of action. Without aid, they die in an hour or two.

If you roll a miss **with a 1 on your action die**, and companion health is 0: **they are dead.** Take 1 experience for each marked ability on the companion asset, then remove the asset from your character.

---

Narrate the companion's state clearly. If they're gravely wounded, make the time pressure real — the player has an hour or two before losing them permanently.
