# Forsake Your Vow

**Trigger:** You renounce your quest, betray your promise, or the goal is lost to you.

**No roll to trigger.** This is a consequence move.

**Mechanics:**
1. Clear the vow progress track.
2. Trigger **Endure Stress** → `moves/endure-stress.md`

Spirit loss equals the rank of the forsaken vow:
| Rank | Spirit loss |
|---|---|
| Troublesome | -1 spirit |
| Dangerous | -2 spirit |
| Formidable | -3 spirit |
| Extreme | -4 spirit |
| Epic | -5 spirit |

If the vow was made to a person or community with whom you share a bond, trigger **Test Your Bond** → `moves/test-your-bond.md` when you next meet.

---

This is a heavy moment. Narrate what breaks inside the character. Let it land before moving on.
